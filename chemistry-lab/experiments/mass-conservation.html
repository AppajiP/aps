Lavoisier's law

In any closed-system chemical reaction the total mass of products equals the total mass of reactants. Atoms are merely rearranged, never created or destroyed.

Why open systems seem to violate the law

If a gaseous product escapes (Na₂CO₃ + HCl releases CO₂) the balance will appear to lose mass. Reseal the flask and the discrepancy disappears.
