Laboratory preparation of carbon dioxide

Drop dilute hydrochloric acid onto marble chips and lead the gas through delivery tubes to test density, solubility, and its reaction with limewater.

Generator & tests

Observations

Gas collected
0 mL
Limewater
clear
Candle test
two candles lit

Open the tap on the funnel to start.

Reaction

CaCO₃ + 2 HCl  →  CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂↑

Why these reagents?

  • Dilute HCl, not H₂SO₄ — the latter forms insoluble CaSO₄ that coats the chips and stops the reaction.
  • Marble chips, not powder — keeps the reaction rate controllable.
  • No heating needed — the reaction is exothermic enough on its own.

Properties tested

  • Density: CO₂ is ~1.5 × denser than air, so it pours from a beaker and snuffs lower candles first.
  • Solubility: CO₂ is slightly soluble — collect by upward displacement of air.
  • Limewater test: CO₂ + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃↓ + H₂O. The clear solution turns milky.
