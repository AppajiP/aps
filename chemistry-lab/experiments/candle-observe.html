Observing a burning candle

Light a candle, hover a beaker and a piece of card to see where the flame is hottest, and identify the combustion products.

Candle stage

Observations log

    Three zones of the flame

    • Outer zone — pale, almost invisible. Complete combustion. Hottest.
    • Middle zone — bright yellow. Incandescent soot particles.
    • Inner core — dim blue/violet. Wax vapour, not yet burning.

    Products of combustion

    Paraffin (C₂₅H₅₂ approx.) reacts with O₂ to give CO₂ + H₂O. The beaker fogs with condensed water, and shaken with limewater it turns milky from CO₂.
