Golden rain — recrystallising PbI₂

Mix lead(II) nitrate with potassium iodide. Heat to redissolve, then let the flask cool — shimmering golden PbI₂ crystals settle slowly.

Flask

State

Empty flask.

The chemistry

Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2 KI → PbI₂↓ + 2 KNO₃

PbI₂ is brilliant yellow and almost insoluble in cold water but noticeably soluble in hot water. When the hot saturated solution is allowed to cool slowly, large platelets crystallise out and flutter down — the "golden rain".

Safety

Lead compounds are toxic. In a real lab they must be handled with gloves and disposed of as hazardous waste.
