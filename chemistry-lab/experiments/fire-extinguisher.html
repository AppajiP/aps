Designing a simple fire extinguisher

Combine sodium hydrogen carbonate with dilute hydrochloric acid inside a sealed bottle. The CO₂ pressure pushes a jet out through the nozzle.

Extinguisher

Observations

Pressure inside
1.0 atm
Jet
no flow
Test fire
unlit

Light a test fire first, then tip the bottle to mix.

The reaction

NaHCO₃ + HCl → NaCl + H₂O + CO₂↑

The CO₂ produced is denser than air, smothers the fire by displacing oxygen, and cools the fuel slightly as it expands out of the nozzle.

Real extinguishers

  • Soda-acid (this one) — for paper / wood (Class A).
  • Liquid CO₂ — for electrical fires.
  • Dry powder (NaHCO₃) — for flammable liquids.
