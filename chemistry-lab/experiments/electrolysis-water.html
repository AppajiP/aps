Composition of water — electrolysis (Hofmann voltameter)

Pass a direct current through dilute acidified water and watch H₂ and O₂ collect over the two electrodes in a 2 : 1 volume ratio.

The experiment

A few drops of sulfuric acid increase the conductivity of pure water, which is otherwise an extremely poor conductor. The acid is not consumed — it merely supplies ions that carry the current.

2 H₂O  →  2 H₂↑ + O₂↑

Electrode reactions

  • Cathode (–): 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻ → 2 H₂
  • Anode (+): 2 H₂O → O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4 e⁻

Identification tests

  • Cathode gas: insert a lit splint — H₂ burns with a small "pop".
  • Anode gas: insert a glowing splint — O₂ reignites it.
