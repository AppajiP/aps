Burning iron wire in oxygen

A coil of fine iron wire glows white and throws bright sparks when lowered into a jar of pure oxygen.

Gas jar

Observations

Wire temperature
25 °C
Spark intensity
none
Phase
ready

Open the jar and ignite the matchstick on the wire tip.

Why iron burns only in pure O₂

In air, only ~21 % of the gas is O₂; the heat released by oxidation is diluted by the rest of the air and the wire merely glows red. In pure oxygen, the reaction is fast enough to melt the iron and throw sparks of molten oxide.

3 Fe + 2 O₂  →  Fe₃O₄

Safety notes

  • Cover the bottom of the jar with sand or water — molten Fe₃O₄ will crack hot glass on impact.
  • Use spiral-wound thin wire so the heat is concentrated.
