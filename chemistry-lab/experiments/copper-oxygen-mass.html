What's happening

2 Cu + O₂ → 2 CuO

Each 64 g of copper combines with 16 g of oxygen to form 80 g of black copper(II) oxide. The mass goes up because oxygen from the air is being incorporated into the solid.

Open vs sealed

In an open crucible the mass increases. In a stoppered flask of air, the total mass stays the same — mass is conserved, but it is redistributed from the gas phase into the solid.
