Preparing oxygen by heating potassium permanganate

Standard inquiry experiment. Heat KMnO₄ in a test tube and collect the oxygen produced by displacement of water.

Theory

Solid potassium permanganate decomposes on heating to give a mixture of potassium manganate, manganese(IV) oxide, and oxygen gas:

2 KMnO₄  →  K₂MnO₄ + MnO₂ + O₂↑

Setup tips

  • Plug the tube mouth with a small wad of cotton wool to stop solid particles entering the delivery tube.
  • Tilt the tube slightly downward so condensed water never runs back onto the hot glass.
  • Heat the solid evenly first, then concentrate the flame.

Why displacement of water?

Oxygen is only sparingly soluble in water, so collecting it over a trough gives a pure, dry-enough sample for ignition tests.

Follow-up questions

  1. Why must the delivery tube be removed from the water before the burner is taken away?
  2. What would happen if no cotton plug were used?
  3. Use the mass remaining to estimate the volume of O₂ released at STP for a 5 g sample.
