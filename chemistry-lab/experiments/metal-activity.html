Activity series of metals

Pick a metal and a solution and observe whether a single-displacement reaction takes place. The more reactive metal displaces the less reactive one from its compound.

Reaction test tube

Observations

Drop a metal into the solution to begin.

Activity series

From most to least reactive (school-level subset):

K > Ca > Na > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > (H) > Cu > Hg > Ag > Pt > Au

Rules of thumb

  • A metal above hydrogen displaces H₂ from dilute acids.
  • A metal higher up displaces a lower one from its salt solution.
  • Cu, Ag, Au do not react with dilute non-oxidising acids.
