Balancing chemical equations

Adjust the coefficients in front of each species until every element has the same total count on both sides of the arrow.

Element tally

How to balance

  1. Count each kind of atom on both sides.
  2. Adjust the integer coefficient (never the subscripts!) in front of a formula to even out the count.
  3. Balance the metals first, then non-metals, then H, then O.
  4. Reduce all coefficients to their lowest whole-number ratio.

The total mass of reactants always equals the total mass of products — this is the law of conservation of mass.
