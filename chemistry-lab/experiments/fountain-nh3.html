The ammonia fountain

A round-bottomed flask full of dry NH₃ is connected to a beaker of water containing phenolphthalein. A single squeeze of water into the flask starts a vivid pink fountain.

Press the rubber dropper to start.

Why the fountain works

Ammonia is exceptionally soluble — about 700 volumes of NH₃ dissolve in one volume of water at room temperature. A single drop of water dissolves enough gas to reduce the pressure inside the flask dramatically, so atmospheric pressure pushes the water up.

NH₃ + H₂O  ⇌  NH₄⁺ + OH⁻

The OH⁻ produced makes phenolphthalein turn pink, so the rising jet glows pink as it fills the flask.
